Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 25, 2026

Overview

Focus Read is a reading aid app for children. We take your privacy seriously — and the simplest way to protect it is to not collect any data at all.

Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

  • No user accounts or login required
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No in-app purchases
  • No data is sent to any server

Local Data

All data (photographs of book pages, recognized text, and settings) is stored exclusively on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

Camera & Photo Library

The app requests access to your camera and photo library solely to photograph or import book pages for text recognition. Photos are processed entirely on your device using on-device OCR technology. No images are uploaded or transmitted.

Children's Privacy

Focus Read is designed for children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. Since we do not collect any data whatsoever, there is no children's data to protect — by design.

Third-Party Services

Focus Read does not use any third-party services, SDKs, or APIs that collect data. All processing (text recognition, text-to-speech) happens on your device using built-in platform capabilities.

Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Given our commitment to collecting no data, significant changes are unlikely.
